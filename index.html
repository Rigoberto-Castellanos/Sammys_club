Sammy's Club

The premier competitive distance running team in the San Fernando Valley. Track workouts, tempo sessions, and long runs for runners chasing PRs.

In Memoriam: Coach Sammy

"Dedicated to the memory of our coach, Ismael 'Sammy' Samano. He was far more than an instructor—he was a mentor and a father figure who built this team from the ground up. He went out of his way to buy food, clothes, and running shoes for athletes who needed them, teaching us what true selflessness and family look like. Though he passed away after a brave battle with cancer, the grit, work ethic, and belief he instilled in us will never fade. Every mile we race is in his honor."

Team Leadership

Rigoberto Castellanos

Founder & Team Captain

As a proud disciple of Coach Sammy, Rigoberto returned to running under his mentorship through grueling 5:00 AM workouts with his dog, Mercy. Overcoming a lung infection to win a city title and racking up over 200 race victories for Monroe, Rigoberto also hosted the AM Runs YouTube channel.

Now, the self-proclaimed "Most Handsome Narcissist" is focused on giving back. He founded Sammy's Club to mentor the next generation of runners and carry his coach's legacy into every stride.

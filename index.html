In Memoriam: Coach Sammy

"Dedicated to the memory of our coach, Ismael 'Sammy' Samano. After leaving his mark as a fierce competitor for Monroe High School, he returned to his roots as an assistant coach to guide the next generation. Sammy was far more than an instructor. He was a mentor and a father figure who built this team's foundation. He went out of his way to buy food, clothes, and running shoes for athletes in need, inspiring the youth by showing what limitless selflessness truly looks like. Coaching memorable runners such as Rigo, Malcolm, and Jorge, his impact shaped our character just as much as our times. Though he passed away after a brave battle with cancer, the grit, work ethic, and family values he instilled will never fade. Every mile we race is in his honor."

Team Members

Rigoberto Castellanos

Co-Founder

After quitting the sport during the 2020 pandemic, Rigoberto was pulled back in by the newly hired Coach Samano, who pushed him to train at 5:00 AM alongside him and his loyal dog, Mercy (who also passed away from cancer in 2025). Driven by Coach Samano's unwavering belief, Rigo went on a historic run, capturing a city title and racking up over 200 career race victories for Monroe. His dominance inspired his role hosting the hit AM Runs YouTube channel, where he dubbed himself "The Most Handsome Narcissist" and claimed to be "cursed by humbleness" after never finding competition strong enough to beat him. Following a rough college career cut short by severe illness that later required him to undergo life-saving surgery, Samano's passing motivated Rigo to get back into the sport just like he would have wanted, dedicating every stride to the coach and father figure who believed in him first.

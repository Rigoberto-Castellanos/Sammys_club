Our Mission

We are a community of dedicated distance runners pushing each other to the next level. Whether you're aiming for a sub-3 marathon or just trying to get faster on the track, you'll find your pace here.

Weekly Workouts

Tuesdays @ 6:00 PM

Track Intervals

Speed sessions, 400m-1600m repeats, and lactate threshold pacing at the local track.

Thursdays @ 6:30 PM

Tempo Run

5 to 7-mile sustained tempo efforts with progressive pace finishes through the neighborhood.

Saturdays @ 7:00 AM

Long Run

10 to 16 miles of rolling hills to build the aerobic base. Multiple pace groups available.

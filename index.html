Sammy's Club

The premier competitive distance running team in the San Fernando Valley. Track workouts, tempo sessions, and long runs for runners chasing PRs.

In Memoriam: Coach Sammy

"Dedicated to the memory of our coach, Ismael 'Sammy' Samano. He first made his mark competing fiercely for Monroe High School before returning to his roots as an assistant coach to guide the next generation. He was far more than an instructor—he was a mentor and a father figure who built this team from the ground up. He went out of his way to buy food, clothes, and running shoes for athletes who needed them, teaching us what true selflessness and family look like. Though he passed away after a brave battle with cancer, the grit, work ethic, and belief he instilled in us will never fade. Every mile we race is in his honor."

Team Leadership

Rigoberto Castellanos

Co-Founder

As a proud disciple of Coach Sammy, Rigoberto returned to running under his mentorship through grueling 5:00 AM workouts with his dog, Mercy. Overcoming an active lung infection to capture a city title, he went on to rack up over 200 race victories for Monroe High School.

Despite an abysmally terrible college running career that he prefers not to talk about, his ego remained fully intact. Building a presence as the self-proclaimed "Most Handsome Narcissist" while hosting the AM Runs YouTube channel, Rigoberto has finally channeled his focus into something bigger than himself. Now back on home soil, he co-founded Sammy's Club to give back to the local running community, mentor the next generation, and carry Coach Samano's fiery legacy forward into every stride.

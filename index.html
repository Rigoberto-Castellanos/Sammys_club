Sammy's Club

The premier competitive distance running team in the San Fernando Valley. Track workouts, tempo sessions, and long runs for runners chasing PRs.

In Memoriam: Coach Sammy

"Dedicated to the memory of our coach. He was far more than an instructor—he was a mentor and a father figure who profoundly shaped our character and our love for the sport. Though he passed away after a brave battle with cancer, his enduring spirit and dedication continue to push us forward. Every mile we race is in his honor."

Team Leadership

Rigoberto Castellanos

Founder & Team Captain

Rigoberto is one of Coach Samano’s proudest disciples. After walking away from the sport during the 2020 pandemic, Coach Sammy refused to let his talent slip away, bringing him back with grueling 5:00 AM workouts alongside his loyal dog, Mercy.

That relentless grit forged a city title victory despite an active lung infection, over 200 race wins for Monroe, and a popular stint hosting the AM Runs YouTube channel as the self-proclaimed "Most Handsome Narcissist." Now back in Los Angeles, Rigoberto founded Sammy's Club to mentor the community and carry his coach's legacy into every stride.

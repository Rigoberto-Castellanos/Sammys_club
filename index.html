In Memorial

Coach Ismael

Mentor, Coach, & Father Figure

"Dedicated in eternal gratitude to our coach, mentor, and father figure. Coach Ismael's legacy began as a runner for James Monroe High School, where he later returned to serve as the assistant coach, guiding notable athletes like Rigo, Malcolm, and Jorge. He was known for his extraordinary, selfless acts of kindness—frequently buying food, clothes, and running shoes for his students out of his own pocket to ensure they could succeed. He forged a profound, close-knit connection with the entire community. Though lost to cancer, his voice, discipline, and boundless generosity stay with us on every interval and starting line. Sammy's Club is founded and run entirely in his honor."

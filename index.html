Our Mission

Sammys Club is committed to serious aerobic development, teamwork, and honoring competitive distance running traditions. We provide structured training blocks, accountability, and a supportive culture for runners targeting track, road, and cross country PRs across the San Fernando Valley.

Meet the Team!

Rigoberto Castellanos

Founder & Team Captain

Brings eight years of competitive distance running experience, including leading as high school cross country and track captain in Los Angeles and competing in collegiate distance running. Rigoberto founded Sammys Club to honor Coach Ismael's legacy, uphold structured workout regimens, and foster a competitive racing culture.

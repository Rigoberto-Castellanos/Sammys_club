In Memoriam: Coach Ismael "Sammy" Samano

"Dedicated to the life, fire, and legacy of Coach Sammy. He was more than a coach—he was a father figure and mentor who rebuilt runners from the ground up and taught us the true meaning of heart. Though he fought a courageous battle with cancer, the grit, work ethic, and belief he instilled in us will never fade. Every workout, every lap, and every race is run in his honor."

Our Mission

Built out of pure dedication to the sport. We are a collective of distance runners pushing each other through hard miles, disciplined pacing, and unmatched camaraderie. No shortcuts, no excuses—just honest work on the track and roads.

Team Leadership

Rigoberto Castellanos

Founder & Team Captain

Rigoberto is one of Coach Samano’s proudest disciples. After walking away from running during the 2020 pandemic, Coach Sammy—newly arrived to the program—refused to let his talent slip away. Month after month, Sammy brought him back into form with unrelenting 5:00 AM workouts alongside his loyal dog Mercy (who sadly passed in 2025 after her own battle with cancer).

That grind paid off: after a year of rebuilding, Rigoberto captured his first city title while battling through an active lung infection. He went on to rack up over 200 race victories for Monroe and built a presence as the self-proclaimed "Most Handsome Narcissist" hosting the popular AM Runs YouTube channel.

Following an unforgiving college running stint, recent events have re-ignited his purpose. Now back on home soil, he founded Sammy's Club to give back to the local running community, mentor the next generation, and carry Coach Samano’s fiery legacy forward into every stride.
